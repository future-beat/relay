# Billing and Plans

Lanekeep offers three plans:

- **Free** — up to 3 projects, 2 members per project, community support.
- **Pro** — $12/user/month, unlimited projects, priority email support, API access.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, SSO/SAML, dedicated support, 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Refunds

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of a charge, once per
12-month period. Annual subscriptions are refundable pro-rata within 30 days.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
Refund requests must be handled by a human billing agent — the support assistant
should escalate them.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Plan changes take effect immediately; charges are pro-rated. Downgrading below
your current usage (e.g. more than 3 projects on Free) locks the excess projects
read-only rather than deleting them.

Read-only projects are never deleted and are not subject to a retention clock.
Upgrading again restores full write access to all of them immediately and
automatically.
